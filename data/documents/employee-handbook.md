# Employee Handbook & Code of Conduct

## 1. Equal Employment Opportunity & Workplace Respect
Our organization is committed to providing a work environment free of discrimination, harassment, and retaliation. All employment decisions are based on business needs, job requirements, and individual qualifications without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, veteran status, or disability status.

## 2. Working Hours and Flexible Schedules
- Core business hours are from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.
- Standard full-time workweeks consist of 40 hours.
- Employees eligible for hybrid work may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager coordination.

## 3. Communication Norms
- Internal team communications are conducted via Slack and email.
- Video meetings require an agenda shared at least 2 hours in advance.
- Official company announcements are distributed via the All-Hands newsletter and monthly town halls.

## 4. Conflict of Interest and Outside Employment
Employees may engage in secondary employment or advisory roles only if such activities do not compete with company offerings, do not utilize company assets or intellectual property, and do not impair job performance during standard working hours. Written disclosure to HR is mandatory before accepting outside engagements.
