Customer Support and Service Level Agreement (SLA) Policy

1. Support Channels and Operating Hours
Customer Support is available through email (support@company.local), live web chat, and dedicated telephone hotlines.
Operating hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern Time.
Emergency critical incident support is available 24/7 for Tier-1 Enterprise clients.

2. Response Time SLAs
- Priority 1 (System Outage / Critical): Initial response within 15 minutes. Hourly status updates until resolution.
- Priority 2 (Major Feature Impairment): Initial response within 2 hours. Resolution target within 8 business hours.
- Priority 3 (Minor Defect / Account Query): Initial response within 8 hours. Resolution target within 24 business hours.
- Priority 4 (General Inquiries / Documentation): Response within 24 business hours.

3. Escalation Procedures
If an issue is not resolved within the defined target resolution window, the ticket is automatically escalated to the Duty Engineering Lead and Support Director.
