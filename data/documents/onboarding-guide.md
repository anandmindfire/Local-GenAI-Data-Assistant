# Employee Onboarding Procedure & Checklist

## 1. Welcome to the Team
The employee onboarding process is designed to support new team members through their first 90 days of employment.

## 2. Day One Checklist
- **Identity & Legal Verification**: Complete Form I-9 / national right-to-work documentation with People Operations.
- **Hardware Issuance**: Collect company-issued laptop, security YubiKey, and monitor from IT Asset Services.
- **Single Sign-On (SSO)**: Activate enterprise Google Workspace account, configure two-factor authentication (2FA) via hardware security key or authenticator app.
- **Buddy Introduction**: Meet your assigned Onboarding Buddy for a welcome coffee and office/virtual tour.

## 3. Week One Objectives
- Attend the Company Overview and Core Values session hosted by Executive Leadership.
- Complete Mandatory Compliance Training modules: Security Awareness, Anti-Harassment, and Data Privacy.
- Review team roadmap and development environment setup guides with your Engineering Manager.

## 4. 30-Day Checkpoint
- Deliver your first starter project / pull request into production.
- Conduct a 30-day alignment 1-on-1 with your reporting manager to review role expectations and initial feedback.
