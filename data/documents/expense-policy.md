# Travel and Business Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. General Principles
Employees incurring legitimate business travel and entertainment expenses on behalf of the company are entitled to reimbursement in accordance with this policy. All expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and accompanied by itemized receipts.

## 2. Airfare and Rail Travel
- Flights under 6 hours must be booked in Economy / Coach class.
- Flights exceeding 6 continuous hours of duration are eligible for Premium Economy booking with prior director-level approval.
- Bookings should be completed at least 14 days prior to departure to secure corporate discounted rates.

## 3. Lodging and Hotel Limits
- Standard business hotels are reimbursed up to $200 per night (exclusive of local taxes) in non-tier-1 cities.
- In tier-1 designated metro areas (e.g., New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo), nightly limits are capped at $300.

## 4. Daily Meal Per Diem
- Meals during active business travel are reimbursed up to a maximum daily allowance of **$75 per day** ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner).
- Alcohol is not eligible for reimbursement unless part of an authorized business dinner with external clients.

## 5. Expense Submission Deadline
Expense reports with scanned receipts must be submitted via the finance portal within 30 days of the expense incurrence date. Claims submitted after 60 days will be rejected.
