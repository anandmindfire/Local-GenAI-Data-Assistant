# Company Leave and Time-Off Policy

## 1. Annual Paid Leave
All full-time employees accrue paid annual leave starting from their official date of hire.
- Full-time employees receive **24 paid leaves annually** (2 days accrued per completed calendar month).
- Part-time employees accrue paid leave on a pro-rata basis aligned with their weekly contracted hours.
- A maximum of 5 unused paid leaves may be carried over into the subsequent calendar year. Any additional unused leaves beyond 5 will lapse on December 31st without financial compensation unless required by local statutory laws.

## 2. Sick and Medical Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick leaves per calendar year for illness, medical appointments, or caregiving for immediate family members.
- Absences exceeding 3 consecutive business days require a medical certificate issued by a licensed healthcare professional.
- Unused sick leaves do not carry over to the following year and are not eligible for cash payout upon termination.

## 3. Parental and Caregiver Leave
- Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth or legal adoption of a child.
- Secondary caregivers are eligible for 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
- Parental leave must be commenced within 12 months of the qualifying birth or adoption date.

## 4. Bereavement Leave
Employees receive up to 5 consecutive paid days off for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and up to 3 days for extended family members.

## 5. Leave Request Procedure
All planned leaves must be submitted via the internal HR portal at least 14 days in advance for manager approval. Emergency leaves must be notified to the reporting manager within 2 hours of the start of the scheduled workday.
