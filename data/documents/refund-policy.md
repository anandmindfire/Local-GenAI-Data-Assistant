# Customer Return and Refund Policy

## 1. 30-Day Standard Return Window
We take pride in our product quality and customer satisfaction. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase:
- Customers may return eligible products within **30 calendar days** of the delivery confirmation date for a full refund or exchange.
- Products must be in original, unused condition, including all original packaging, manuals, power adapters, and accessories.

## 2. Non-Refundable and Final Sale Items
The following categories are strictly non-refundable:
- Downloadable software licenses, digital gift cards, and consumed subscriptions.
- Personalized, custom-engraved, or bespoke hardware configurations.
- Products marked explicitly as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" at checkout.
- Opened consumable items such as screen cleaning kits or thermal paste.

## 3. Defective, Damaged, or Incomplete Items
If an item arrives damaged in transit or exhibits manufacturing defects:
- The customer must notify Customer Support within 48 hours of delivery with photographic evidence and the order ID.
- We provide prepaid return shipping labels for all verified defective goods.
- Customers may choose between an immediate replacement shipment or a 100% refund to the original payment method.

## 4. Refund Processing Timeline
Once our warehouse inspects the returned merchandise:
- Refund approval or denial is determined within **2 business days** of parcel receipt.
- Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original method of payment (credit card, debit card, or electronic bank transfer).
- Financial institutions typically take 3 to 5 business days to reflect the credit on the customer's account balance.
- Shipping fees paid on the original order are non-refundable unless the return is due to our shipping error or product defect.

## 5. Price Match Guarantee Adjustments
If an item purchased from our store drops in price within 14 days of purchase, customers may request a partial refund for the price difference via Customer Support.
